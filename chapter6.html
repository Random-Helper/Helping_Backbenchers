The Data Science Squad

This chapter is basically about AI and data science

AI is the intelligence showed by machines.

Domains of AI

  1. Data: the information that a machine can use.
  2. Computer Vision: helps digital systems understand and process images.
  3. Natural Language Processing: it allows machines to understand human language.

Forms of Data

  • Text: Written information such as books, websites, and articles.
  • Audio: Sound information such as songs, speeches, and news.
  • Image: Visual information such as pictures and scanned documents.
  • Video: Moving visual information such as movies and video clips.

Types of Data

Data is mainly divided into quantitative and qualitative data.

Quantitative Data

Quantitative data: Data that can be represented using numbers.

Examples: Age, height, weight, temperature, and salary.

Qualitative Data

Qualitative data: Data that describes qualities or characteristics.

Examples: Behaviour, appearance, quality, and reviews.

Continuous Data

Continuous data: Numerical data that can have any value within a range.

Examples: Temperature, weight, and salary.

Discrete Data

Discrete data: Numerical data that has fixed, countable values.

Examples: Number of students, siblings, printers, or balls.

What is Data Science?

Data Science: A field that uses data, tools, statistics, and technology to find useful information and make decisions.

Life Cycle of Data Science

Capture

Capture: Collecting raw data from different sources.

Maintain

Maintain: Storing, cleaning, and organising data so it can be used properly.

Process

Process: Preparing data by finding patterns and organising it.

Analyse

Analyse: Studying data to find useful information or insights.

Communicate

Communicate: Presenting the results using graphs, reports, or other visualisations.

Applications of Data Science

Banking

Banking: Data science helps banks detect fraud, recommend loans, and understand customers.

Healthcare

Healthcare: Data science helps doctors examine patients, detect diseases, and monitor health conditions.

E-Commerce

E-Commerce: Data science helps online stores recommend products based on users' searches and purchases.

Transport

Transport: Data science helps self-driving cars and GPS systems make decisions using data.
